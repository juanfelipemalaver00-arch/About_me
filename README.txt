## My Approach to Data

I am particularly interested in the process that happens between:

**Raw Data → Statistical Model → Insight → Decision**

Good analytical work is not only about building a model with strong predictive performance. It is also about understanding the data-generating process, choosing appropriate methodology, validating assumptions, communicating uncertainty, and translating results into decisions.

That is why my projects often combine **econometrics, statistics, machine learning, programming, and business strategy**.
